# Speakery Terms Of Use Draft

Last updated: 2026-07-02

## Service

Speakery provides English learning content, practice tools, social profile features, and AI-assisted feedback.

## Accounts

Users are responsible for keeping their account secure and for using accurate, respectful profile information.

## Acceptable Use

Users must not:

- Harass other users.
- Upload illegal, harmful, or abusive content.
- Attempt to bypass payment, security, or admin restrictions.
- Abuse AI endpoints or automate excessive requests.

## AI Feedback

AI feedback is educational support and may be imperfect. Users should review outputs and not treat them as professional, legal, medical, or financial advice.

## Content

Speakery owns or licenses the app content, design, and learning materials. Users may use the content for personal learning.

## Termination

Speakery may suspend accounts that violate these terms or create security, abuse, or payment risk.
